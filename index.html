    About Mad Orbs

    Mad Orbs is a free online shooter that runs in your browser. You roll an armed Orb around an arena seen from above and fight other players in real time: up to 16 per match, in Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch or Capture the Flag. There is nothing to download or install, and you can play as a guest or sign in with Google to keep your name, your player ID, your stats and your place in the ranking.

    It is a remake of Babo Violent 2, a cult indie shooter released by RndLabs in 2006. The physics, the inertia of the Orbs, the counterweight aiming, the weapons and their damage come from the original source code, which its creators released as free software. On top of that classic core, Mad Orbs adds a modern interface, an endless survival mode with bosses and permanent upgrades, private rooms for friends and the game in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

    How to play

    1. Choose your OrbPick a style and three colours, then type your name. Your Orb is kept in this browser and, if you sign in, in your account.
    2. Pick a mode and press Play nowYou join the online match of that mode with whoever is playing. When the rooms fill up, another one opens by itself; with few people, bots fill the match until more arrive.
    3. Move and shootW, A, S and D roll the Orb, which keeps its momentum; the mouse aims and the left button shoots. The right button throws a grenade, the middle one a molotov, and Space uses your secondary weapon: knives, the nuke bot or the shield.
    4. Choose your weaponsEsc opens the weapon menu: sub machine gun, shotgun, sniper rifle, dual machine gun, chain gun, bazooka, photon rifle or flame thrower. The choice applies the next time you respawn, and F swaps your weapon for one lying on the floor.
    5. Keep an eye on the matchTab shows the scoreboard, T opens the chat and the minimap in the corner shows the walls and where the fight is. Kill streaks are announced: Double Kill, Triple Kill, Dominating...

    Game modes

    Deathmatch

    Everyone against everyone. Each kill is a point, and the first to reach the goal wins the match. The best mode to learn the weapons and the movement.

    Team Deathmatch

    Blue team against red team, balanced by the game. Every kill counts for your team, and you can't hurt your teammates.

    Capture the Flag

    Each team has a flag at its base. Grab the enemy flag and carry it to your own base, with yours at home, to score a capture. A carrier who dies drops it, and its team returns it by touching it.

    Waves

    Survive endless waves of bots that hunt you down. Break crates for power-ups and face a boss every ten waves. Every run earns Cores for upgrades that stay, and milestones unlock starting weapons and exclusive Orbs.

    Training

    You and the bots, on any map and in any mode, with no internet needed. Choose how many bots and how well they play.

    Questions

    Is Mad Orbs free?
    Yes. Everything is free, online and offline. The game shows a few ads on the start screen and in the pauses, never while your Orb is alive in a match.
    Do I need to download anything?
    No. Mad Orbs runs in the browser, on Windows, Mac and Linux. It works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.
    Does it work on phones?
    Not yet: the game needs a keyboard and a mouse. Open madorbs.com on a computer to play.
    How do I play with my friends?
    Create a private room, choose the mode and the map, and send the link or the six-letter code. You need an account to create a room; your friends can join as guests.
    Do I need an account?
    No, guests play every mode. With an account (Sign in, with Google) you keep a permanent player ID, your Orb, your stats in each mode, your Survival progress and your place in the ranking.
    What is Babo Violent 2?
    A multiplayer shooter seen from above, released in 2006 by RndLabs and remembered for its fast matches and its peculiar physics. Its creators later published the source code, and Mad Orbs was rebuilt from it to bring the game back, now in the browser.
