Campaign

You alone against the bots, level by level

The campaign is the mode to play alone, at your own pace: levels against bots that run in your own browser, with no need for anyone online. On the start screen, click Campaign and pick a chapter and a level. Each level is a team match: you alone on the blue team, the bots on the red one.

The rules

  • Dying is losing. You have one life: go down, the level ends and you try again.
  • The bots don't respawn either. The level is won when the last bot goes down.
  • Each chapter has one weapon. You and the bots use only it, plus the knives and the grenades.
  • Each level has one more bot: 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 bots in the chapter's five levels.
  • Each chapter has smarter bots: they aim better and react faster. At first you also take less: half the damage in chapter 1, a little more each chapter.
  • Two health packs lie around the map. Each gives back half your life; when one is taken, another shows up somewhere else 30 seconds later. The bots can take them too.

The boss

The last level of each chapter is a boss: a bigger orb, 5 times tougher, with its life in a bar at the top of the screen. It doesn't come alone: it starts with two guards, and while it lives a new guard shows up now and then. To win, take the boss down.

Stars

The stars come from your time: the faster you clear the level, the more stars. While you play, the stars you can still get show under the clock, with the time limit of the next one. In the first chapter, for example, the level with 1 bot gives ★★★ up to 0:19 and ★★ up to 0:36; the boss gives ★★★ up to 1:04 and ★★ up to 1:56. Slower weapons, like the sniper and the bazooka, get a bit more time.

Your progress

The levels you won and your best time in each are kept in your browser. If you sign in with your Google account (the Sign in button at the top of the start screen), the progress is also kept in your account: you can go on from any computer, and what you played without an account comes along.

Tips

  • With several bots, don't fight in the middle of them: use the walls to take them one at a time.
  • The bots drop a health pack when they go down. Pick it up before going for the next one.
  • Against the boss, deal with the guards first and attack the boss when it's alone.
  • To learn each weapon, see the weapons guide. The other modes are in Game modes.
