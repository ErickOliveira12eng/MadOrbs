Mad Orbs - game data

The files in this folder are original Babo Violent 2 content (RndLabs, 2006): models, textures,
orb skins, sounds, music and maps. David St-Louis ("Daivuk"), one of the game's creators, published
that content in the game's source code repository, https://github.com/Daivuk/BaboViolent2 (commit
a6596d1, "Merged Engine + Added content", 7 December 2012), which is licensed under the GNU General
Public License, version 3 (LICENSE.txt in that repository). Mad Orbs uses these files under that
license.

Two changes: in models/LifePack.DKO the cross is green instead of red (the red cross on white is a
protected emblem), and skins/skin16.tga says "MAD ORBS" where it said "RndLabs". Files of the original folder that the game doesn't use were left out.

Exception: sounds/announcer/ (the announcer's voice) is "WARLORD Announcer Audio Pack" by
VoiceBosch, https://voicebosch.itch.io/warlord-announcer-audio-pack, under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (CC BY-SA 4.0); only the file names were changed.
See sounds/announcer/LICENSE.txt.

Exception: the music is by GTDStudio (Palrom), from OpenGameArt.org:
- sounds/Menu.ogg: "NИNN (metal)", https://opengameart.org/content/n%D0%B8nn-metal, under the Creative
  Commons Attribution 3.0 license (CC BY 3.0).
- sounds/Music.ogg: "Doom", "Skirmish" and "Dirby day" from "Triple kill (multiple tracks)",
  https://opengameart.org/content/triple-kill-multiple-tracks, under the OGA-BY 3.0 license
  (https://opengameart.org/content/oga-by-30-faq), played one after the other.
Changes: the volume was lowered, the three tracks were joined into one loop, the menu track lost its
first tenth of a second of silence, and both were encoded as Ogg Vorbis.

ORIGINAL-LICENSE.txt is the end-user license that shipped with the 2006 freeware game; it is part
of the content folder as it was published.
