Waves

Survive for as long as you can

Waves is the survival mode of Mad Orbs: you alone against wave after wave of bots, with no end. It runs in your own browser, with no server and no other players, on the map CTF-Invaders. On the start screen, click the red Waves card. The goal is simple: get as far as you can. Your record is the highest wave you reached, and it goes to the ranking.

And no run is wasted: each one earns Cores, which you trade in the Workshop for upgrades that stay for good. Every run starts a little stronger than the one before, like in a roguelite.

The rules

  • Three lives (up to five with the Workshop). When you go down you are back by yourself 5 seconds later, while you have lives left. The clock of the wave stops while you are dead.
  • You start with the sub machine gun (or the weapon a milestone gave you), the knives, 2 grenades and a molotov. Better weapons come from the crates.
  • Each wave has a time limit: 30 seconds plus one per wave, up to a minute. When it runs out the next wave comes even if enemies are still alive, and they stay on the map.
  • A short break between waves: 9 seconds (5 before the first one).
  • The bots hunt you. They always know where you are and come for you, a little slower than you (70% of your speed). They don't break crates on purpose and don't take the power-ups.
  • Life packs: only about one fallen bot in three drops one, and it gives back a quarter of your life (the boss always drops one).
  • They hit softer than in the other modes, a quarter of the damage, but every wave they hit a little harder (1.5% per wave) and take a little longer to go down: about 31% tougher on wave 10, twice as tough on wave 30.
  • More bots every wave: 3 on the first one, 12 on the tenth, 25 on the twentieth. They come in a few at a time, and their aim gets better slowly: the first waves are there to get the hang of it.
  • Their weapons change: only sub machine guns at first, then shotguns from wave 4, dual machine guns from wave 7, chain guns from wave 10, sniper rifles from wave 13, bazookas from wave 16, and flame throwers and photon rifles from wave 20.
  • A boss every ten waves: the eye, a big orb that takes a quarter of the damage, with fewer bots around it.

Crates and power-ups

Wooden crates appear on the map: three new ones at each break and one more every 20 seconds during a wave, up to six at once. They stop bullets and rockets, so they are also cover. Shoot them, blow them up or hit them with the knives: each one drops a power-up that waits 30 seconds on the floor. Roll over it to take it.

  • Health: gives back half your life.
  • Grenades: 2 grenades and a molotov more.
  • New weapon: a stronger weapon falls on the floor. Press F over it to take it; it is yours until you go down.
  • Speed, rapid fire, shield and fury last 15 seconds: 35% faster, 60% more shots, half the damage taken and double damage.
  • Extra life: one more life.
  • Bomb: a blast around you that hurts every enemy in sight within 6 blocks and breaks the crates nearby.

Some power-ups are permanent: they last until the end of the run, and each one you take adds a level.

  • Fire rate, damage and speed: 10% more per level.
  • Resistance: 10% less damage taken per level.
  • Poison shot: whoever you hit keeps losing life for 3 seconds, and the kill is yours.
  • Ice shot: whoever you hit slows down for a second and a half.

Cores and the Workshop

Every run ends with Cores, even the worst one. Each wave you survive gives 3 Cores plus one for every two waves of its number (higher waves are worth more), every 5 kills give 1, each boss beaten gives 25 and a new record 30 more. Leaving in the middle of a run counts too. Before you play, the Workshop (the Waves card on the start screen opens it) trades the Cores for permanent upgrades, with several levels each:

  • Health: 10% more per level (you take more hits).
  • Damage and fire rate: 5% more per level; speed: 4%.
  • Starting lives: one more life per level, up to five.
  • Grenades: one more grenade each time you spawn.
  • Crate luck: 10% more chance per level that a crate drops a permanent power-up.
  • Breather: some health back after each wave cleared (20%, 35% and 50%).
  • Second wind: once per run, a blow that would kill you leaves you with half your health.

The Workshop also has a daily reward: come back every day for free Cores, more each day in a row (from 20 on the first to 100 on the seventh); miss a day and the week starts over.

The Cores and the upgrades stay in your browser and, if you sign in with Google, in your account, on any device.

Milestones, exclusive Orbs and checkpoints

Some waves are milestones: reach each one once and its prize is yours for good. On wave 10, the shotgun to start a run with; on 15, the exclusive Honeycomb Orb; on 20, the chain gun; on 25, the Flames Orb; on 30, the bazooka; on 40, the flame thrower; and on 50, the Crown Orb. The exclusive Orbs show up in the Orb picker of the start screen and work in every mode, online too.

Every 10 waves you reach also give you a checkpoint: in the Workshop, choose to start on wave 10, 20, 30... Starting further on gives you one permanent run power-up for every two waves skipped, but the skipped waves give no Cores.

Your record and the ranking

The record is the wave you were fighting when your last life went, with your kills as the tiebreak. It is kept in your browser and, if you sign in with Google, in your account; the Ranking on the start screen has a Waves tab with the best runs. Esc pauses the run whenever you need.

Tips

  • Keep moving. The bots come straight at you, so a loop around the blocks of the map keeps them in a line behind you, where every shot finds someone.
  • In the Workshop, health and speed are good first buys: they keep you alive for more waves, and more waves mean more Cores.
  • Use the breaks to break crates and pick up what they drop before the next wave arrives.
  • Take the permanent power-ups early: a few levels of fire rate and resistance make the late waves possible.
  • Throw grenades where the bots come in, while they are still bunched up.
  • Keep the bomb and the fury for the boss waves or for when the map is full.
  • A crate between you and a sniper is a wall: let it take the shots while you deal with the closest bots.
